## Start with free credits

New DigitalOcean accounts get a [signup credit](https://docs.digitalocean.com/platform/billing/signup-credit/):

- **$5 of credit**, applied automatically when you create your first team. No promo code is needed.
- It expires **90 days** after signup and applies to all products except SaaS Add-Ons, so it covers App Platform.
- You must add a valid payment method before you can create any resource.
- The official pages checked list no standing offer above $5. Larger amounts, when they exist, come from promo codes or campaigns.

Checked on 2 October 2026 on the [signup credit page](https://docs.digitalocean.com/platform/billing/signup-credit/) (last verified by DigitalOcean on 15 July 2026). Offers change: check the linked page before you sign up.

## Deploy

No procedure for this platform has been tested yet. What any deployment needs,
taken from the [Docker Compose](/deployment/dockercompose) stack:

- the published images [`apowerb/apowerb`](https://hub.docker.com/r/apowerb/apowerb)
  (API, port `8000`) and [`apowerb/apowerb-ui`](https://hub.docker.com/r/apowerb/apowerb-ui)
  (interface, port `3000`), at the tags pinned together in
  [`docker-compose/docker-compose.yml`](https://github.com/apowerb/apowerb-hosting/blob/main/docker-compose/docker-compose.yml);
  nothing has to be built;
- a PostgreSQL database, passed to the API as `DB_HOST`, `DB_PORT`, `DB_NAME`,
  `DB_USER`, `DB_PASSWORD`;
- `ENCRYPT_KEY`, a Fernet key: `openssl rand -base64 32 | tr '+/' '-_'`;
- `API_URL` on the interface, set to the API's internal address.

For a managed platform that runs a Compose file, the
[Hostman](/deployment/hostman) page is the closest tested reference.
