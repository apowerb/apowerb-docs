## Start with free credits

New DigitalOcean accounts get a [signup credit](https://docs.digitalocean.com/platform/billing/signup-credit/):

- **$5 of credit**, applied automatically when you create your first team. No promo code is needed.
- It expires **90 days** after signup and applies to all products except SaaS Add-Ons, so it covers App Platform.
- You must add a valid payment method before you can create any resource.
- The official pages checked list no standing offer above $5. Larger amounts, when they exist, come from promo codes or campaigns.

Checked on 2 October 2026 on the [signup credit page](https://docs.digitalocean.com/platform/billing/signup-credit/) (last verified by DigitalOcean on 15 July 2026). Offers change: check the linked page before you sign up.

## Deploy

To deploy apowerb to DigitalOcean, you can use the App Platform.

1.  Connect your GitHub repository.
2.  Select the `Dockerfile` from the root of your project.
3.  Configure the environment variables.
4.  Deploy.
